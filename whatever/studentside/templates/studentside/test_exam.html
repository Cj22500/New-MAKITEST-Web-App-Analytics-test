How Monitoring Works

Camera frames are sent to the server to estimate head movement, eye gaze, and face visibility. Selected keyboard events, window focus changes, and hidden tabs are also reported.

Event logs, gaze heatmaps, and clips around flagged events are saved for authorized teacher review. Your microphone is not used.

Monitoring supports human review. These signals alone do not prove misconduct.
